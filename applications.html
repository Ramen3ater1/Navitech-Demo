Applications

Engineered for outdoor and professional applications — wherever performance and protection can't compromise.

01 · Consumer Textiles

Advanced nanospun textiles for everyday performance.

We serve outdoor, lifestyle and fashion brands — anywhere comfort, style and protection need to coexist without compromise.

Sports & performance apparel

Sweat, weather and motion demand materials that adapt. Navitex Membrane manages moisture, blocks wind and rain, and maintains breathability — so athletes and enthusiasts can perform at their peak.

Outdoor & outerwear

From hiking trails to city streets, our membranes keep garments light, breathable and weather-resistant. With outstanding comfort and stretch, Navitex-based fabrics are built for movement in all conditions.

Lifestyle & fashion

Consumers want performance woven seamlessly into everyday clothing. Our nanofiber membranes deliver invisible protection — soft, silent and versatile — supporting designs that feel as good as they look.

02 · Professional Textiles

Cutting-edge nanofiber solutions for professional fields.

We develop and manufacture membranes for performance textiles in firefighting, defense, workwear and beyond.

Workwear

When tasks take you into harsh conditions — rain, chemicals, high heat, abrasion — traditional fabrics struggle. Navitex Membrane is engineered to repel liquids, breathe deeply and stay durable through active, demanding work.

Protective equipment

From boots and outer layers to protective gloves and load-bearing equipment, we design membranes that perform in the field: resisting moisture, wind and wear — without compromising mobility or weight.

Defense & tactical gear

Gear must protect during crises — flash fires, hazardous materials, weather extremes — while never slowing the wearer down. Our nanofiber technologies deliver reliable protection, breathability and agility when it matters most.

03 · Industrial Applications

Beyond textiles — anywhere precise filtration or protection is needed.

From compost covers in agriculture to air and water filtration, construction-grade insulation and home textiles, electrospun nanofiber technology keeps opening new applications.

Agricultural Membrane Covers

Breathable covers that support composting and odor control in agriculture.

Filtration Systems

High-efficiency air and water filtration built on tunable nanofiber structures.

Construction and Furniture

Construction-grade insulation and home textiles that benefit from precise, lightweight membrane engineering.

Navitex Membrane

Ultra-breathable. Waterproof. PFAS-free. No compromise.

Conventional PFAS-free membranes force a trade-off between waterproofness and breathability. Navitex Membrane escapes that trade-off with ultra-fine nanofibres and tunable structures — exceptional performance across a wide range of commercial applications, at industrial scale.
