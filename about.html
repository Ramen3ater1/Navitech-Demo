Spun from a passion for materials into industry leadership.

Our story begins with a long research legacy in electrospinning — two decades of hands-on experimentation that grew into a conviction: nanofiber technology could change the products people rely on every day.

That conviction became a company. Founded by a small team of researchers and engineers, Navitech has grown from a single laboratory into an organization with offices and production across multiple regions. Today the mission is global: to weave nanofiber science into the fabric of everyday life — making products lighter, safer, more sustainable and more comfortable.

Thinner than a single wavelength of light.

Electrospinning gives us unprecedented control at the nanoscale, creating materials finer than a bacterium — as thin as a wavelength of visible light. By tuning fibre diameter, membrane thickness and pore structure, we build materials that breathe more, weigh less and perform better.

Our Technology

Electrospinning at commercial scale.

Electrospinning uses high-voltage electricity to draw polymer solutions into nanofibres — from nanometers to micrometers in diameter — forming highly porous nonwoven membranes. Mastering this process at commercial scale is what unlocks highly functional, ultra-breathable products.

Navitex Membrane

Ultra-breathable. Waterproof. PFAS-free. No compromise.

Conventional PFAS-free membranes force a trade-off: monolithic designs maximize waterproofness at the expense of breathability, while typical microporous designs do the opposite. Navitex Membrane is engineered to escape that trade-off — ultra-fine nanofibres with tunable structures and properties, delivering exceptional performance across a wide range of commercial applications at industrial scale.
