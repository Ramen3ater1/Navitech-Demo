The Technology

Electrospun science, made commercial.

Our patented electrospinning platform manufactures ultra-fine polymer fibres at industrial scale. Tuning fibre diameter, membrane thickness and pore structure at the nanoscale lets us engineer materials a category beyond conventional production.

  • Ultra-breathable, with waterproof and windproof performance in one layer
  • Lightweight — adds almost no bulk to a garment or product
  • Stretchable, conformable and durable laminates for demanding use
  • Responsible by design, with no PFAS chemistry
  • Custom-engineered for unique material challenges, in close collaboration with clients
